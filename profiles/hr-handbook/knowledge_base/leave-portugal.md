# Leave Rules — Portugal

## Vacation Days

Employees in Portugal get 25 working days of vacation per year (22 statutory plus 3
company days). Up to 5 unused days can be carried over until 30 April.

## Public Holidays

The 13 national public holidays plus the municipal holiday of the office city apply.
