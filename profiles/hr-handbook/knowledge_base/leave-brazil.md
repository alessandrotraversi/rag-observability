# Leave Rules — Brazil

## Vacation Days

Employees in Brazil get 30 calendar days of vacation per year, which may be split in
up to three periods, one of them of at least 14 days.

## Vacation Bonus

The constitutional one-third vacation bonus is paid together with the vacation salary.
