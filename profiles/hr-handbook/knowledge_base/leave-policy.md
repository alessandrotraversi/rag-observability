# Leave Policy

All figures are fictional and exist only to demonstrate the RAG pipeline.

## Vacation

Employees accrue vacation monthly. Requests must be submitted in the HR portal at
least 15 days in advance and approved by the direct manager.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave up to 2 consecutive days needs no certificate. From the third day on, a
medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within 48 hours.

## Parental Leave

Acme Labs grants 20 weeks of paid parental leave to every parent, regardless of
gender, on top of the statutory minimum of each country.
