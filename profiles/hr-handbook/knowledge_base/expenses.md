# Travel and Expenses Policy

## Reimbursement Deadline

Expenses must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of the purchase date.

## Local Transport

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed for business trips, client visits and when
leaving the office after 10 pm. Commuting is not reimbursed.

## Meals

Business meals with clients are reimbursed up to the per-person limit set by the
country finance team, with the attendees listed on the claim.
