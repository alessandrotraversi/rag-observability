# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid Model

Teams work in a hybrid model with a minimum of 2 office days per week, agreed per
team. Fully remote contracts need VP approval.

## Home-Office Allowance

A monthly home-office allowance of 50 (local currency units, fictional) is paid with
the salary to every employee with at least one remote day per week.
